Microphone access

Chrome will only let the extension record your microphone after you have granted permission once, and that prompt cannot appear from the background page where capture runs. Grant it here before your first call.

Desktop app Must match the --port Kobayashi is listening on. Only needed if Kobayashi was started with --token.
